01 / About Me

Engineering with a hands-on approach.

I am an Electrical Engineer with a Bachelor of Science in Electronic Systems Engineering Technology from Texas A&M University, where I graduated in May 2026.

My background combines electrical engineering fundamentals with hands-on experience in industrial equipment, electronics, embedded systems, PCB design, instrumentation, and hardware testing.

In industrial environments, I have worked with 380/480V electrical systems, AC/DC motors, transformers, contactors, overload protection, variable frequency drives, control panels, and industrial sensors. I also have exposure to PLC systems, I/O troubleshooting, and automation technologies.

I currently work as an Electrical Engineer at Jindal Pipe USA, supporting industrial electrical maintenance, equipment troubleshooting, and reliability while continuing to develop my skills in controls and automation.

Beyond engineering, I enjoy music, playing instruments, soccer, video production, and community activities.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Engineering a compact tracking solution

This Texas A&M engineering capstone explored a compact tracking platform designed around size, power consumption, wireless communication, and practical deployment requirements.

The proposed system combined GPS positioning, Bluetooth Low Energy communication, an iPhone gateway, and an ESP32-S3 hub with a real-time clock.

GPS BLE ESP32-S3 Embedded Systems PCB
01 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Wireless communication

GPS positioning and Bluetooth Low Energy formed the foundation of the tracking concept. The architecture included an iPhone BLE gateway and an ESP32-S3 hub with an RTC.

02 / HARDWARE DEVELOPMENT

Embedded electronics

The project involved compact electronic hardware, PCB integration, component selection, and consideration of power requirements for a miniaturized tracking device.
