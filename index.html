ECOTRACE / HARDWARE DEVELOPMENT
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Engineering a compact tracking solution

This Texas A&M engineering capstone focused on developing a compact wildlife-tracking platform, progressing from an initial breadboard proof of concept toward dedicated embedded hardware.

The initial concept was tested using an ESP32-S3 on a breadboard. The dedicated tracker was then designed around an nRF52840, GNSS receiver, BMI270 accelerometer, SPI flash storage, LiPo battery, and Bluetooth Low Energy. An ESP32-based gateway received tracker data over BLE and forwarded it.

nRF52840 GNSS BMI270 BLE ESP32 Gateway PCB Design

A future development objective was to pursue integration with Apple's Find My network, similar to the tracking experience used by AirTag, after establishing a working prototype. This integration was a planned direction, not an implemented feature.

01 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Wireless communication

The ESP32-S3 breadboard prototype was used to validate the initial tracking concept. The dedicated nRF52840 tracker communicated over BLE with an ESP32-based gateway, which received and forwarded the tracker data.

02 / HARDWARE DEVELOPMENT

Embedded electronics

The dedicated hardware design incorporated an nRF52840, GNSS receiver, BMI270 accelerometer, SPI flash, and LiPo battery. Development also involved component selection, PCB design, interface integration, and power considerations.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION
